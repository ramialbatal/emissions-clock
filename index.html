How this is calculated

Official inventories report emissions per year, not per second. When this page opens it downloads the latest annual figures (currently up to ), projects them to using each country's latest year-on-year change (→), and spreads the result evenly over the year:

growth   = E[latest] / E[latest − 1] − 1
annual   = E[latest] × (1 + growth)^(this year − latest)
per sec  = annual ÷ seconds in this year
so far   = annual × fraction of the year elapsed (UTC)

Real emissions vary by season, weekday and time of day, so the live counters are a model, not a measurement. Figures are territorial fossil CO₂ including cement; land-use change is excluded, and international aviation and shipping only appear in the world total. Germany is shown separately but is also part of the EU.

The small line chart on each card shows that country's official annual emissions from to , with the dot marking the latest year and the percentage giving the change over the whole period. Each chart is scaled to its own minimum and maximum (it does not start at zero), so compare the shape of the trend rather than the heights between countries. Tap Yearly data (or the chart) to see the values for each year.
