How this is calculated

Official inventories report emissions per year, not per second. This clock takes the latest annual figure (base year ), projects it to using a recent average growth rate, and spreads it evenly over the year:

annual = base × (1 + growth)(year − base year)
per second = annual ÷ seconds in year
so far = annual × fraction of the year elapsed (UTC)

Real emissions vary by season, weekday and time of day, so the live numbers are a model, not a measurement. Figures cover fossil fuels and cement; land-use change is excluded. Germany is shown separately but is also included in the EU total.
